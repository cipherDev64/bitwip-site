01The office

Bitwip in Guadeloupe

The Guadeloupe office opened in 2024 at 1731 rue Henri Becquerel in Baie-Mahault.

For Magasins U Guadeloupe, the team built the website, structured the catalog and supported the brand’s social media communication. For Orchestra, it designed an omnichannel e-commerce site: children’s fashion, baby gear, baby registry and Club Card loyalty program. It also relaunched the Google Ads campaigns for Pièces Auto Web Guadeloupe and created the landing page for the DRANE Guadeloupe communication campaign.

Along with French Guiana and Martinique, Guadeloupe is one of the territories covered by the Solidarité BTP portal, which brings together five social protection and support organizations for the construction industry.

Social media, Google Ads, local billboards, influencer marketing: we design them for audiences in Guadeloupe and fine-tune them continuously, backed by a dashboard.
