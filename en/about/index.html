Got questions?

How long has Bitwip been around, and who runs it?

Since 2012. It all started with a promise Vincent Reboul had made to himself: to start his own company on his 30th birthday. Bitwip was born in French Guiana developing mobile apps, still rare at the time, then became a 360° communications agency in 2018. It is run by three partners: Vincent Reboul, founder and CEO, Terry Geoffroy, business development director and partner, and Christian Nhô, agency director and partner. Read Bitwip’s story.

What does Bitwip do?

Four complementary services. Strategy and consulting: audit, communication strategy, media plan, brand platform. Brand and creative: visual identity, UX/UI design, photo and video production, motion design. Web and digital: websites, e-commerce, web and mobile apps, AI-driven digital transformation. Campaigns and media: media buying, social ads and Google Ads, SEO, social media. See our services.

What labels and certifications has Bitwip earned?

In May 2023, Bitwip became the first agency in Overseas France to earn the “Engagé RSE” (Committed to CSR) label from AFNOR Certification, at the Confirmed level; the label was renewed in 2025. The agency is also a Meta Partner, Google Partner and Dust Solution Partner, an AI solutions provider for SMEs and mid-sized companies, a France Num Activator, a member of FEDOM and a partner of ACCDOM. Our commitments.

Is Bitwip part of a group?

Yes. Bitwip is the communications agency of a group built around the consulting firm Bitwip Conseil, which supports organizations in their strategic choices, their growth and their impact-driven transformations. The group also includes the agency Bitwip IA, which helps companies and institutions adopt artificial intelligence in ways that are useful, ethical and responsible, and the growth accelerator Morpho, which helps business leaders structure their operations and steer their growth.
