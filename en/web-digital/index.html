02Method

One method, five steps

A brand, a campaign or a website: the path is the same, and you always know which step we’re at.

One method, five steps

  1. 01

    Step 1: Scoping

    We look for the problem to solve before the technology.

    Who will use your website or app, and to do what? For an AI project, we start with what you do, then with your data.

    We listen to your goals, challenge them and put them in writing.

    A kickoff meeting: your goals, your audiences, your timeline and budget constraints. If the situation calls for it, an audit of what already exists before any recommendation.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Strategy

    We set the site map and the technical choices.

    Which pages, for which visitor, on which platform: you decide before any mockup.

    A costed, prioritized recommendation you can decide on.

    Our people work together to design the strategy and the action plan.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Creative

    Mockups, copy, then development.

    You approve the mockups for each page before development begins.

    In-house production, with clear approval points.

    Website, branding, communication campaigns: this is where your project comes to life.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Rollout

    Launch, hosting, then handover.

    Your team learns to update it without us.

    Launch, distribution, partner coordination.

    Regular check-ins until launch.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Measurement & review

    Visits, inquiries, sales: the site is measured.

    Tracking starts with the site. You see which pages are read, which ones bring in inquiries, and which ones need rework.

    Results analyzed, adjustments proposed.

    Even after delivery, we look back. At the end of the engagement, we walk you through your project review.

Which CMS should you choose for a showcase website?

There is no single answer: WordPress, Webflow or a static HTML site. The choice depends on your needs, how often you update, your budget and your specifications. A site you edit every week doesn’t call for the same tool as a five-page brochure site that never changes.

Can you manage your website yourself once it’s built?

Yes. WordPress, Webflow, Shopify and PrestaShop let you edit your content without coding. Hands-on training, like the one Bitwip offers, makes you self-sufficient from launch.

Where are your sites hosted?

The WordPress sites we host run on Google Cloud infrastructure in Europe. The most resource-intensive ones run on a dedicated server in a data center in Paris. Webflow and Shopify sites are hosted by their own platforms.

How should a business in Overseas France start an AI project?

With a business audit: map the processes, identify friction points and quantify potential gains, before any technical question. Then come the data audit and change management, without losing sight of the ways AI can go wrong. The Bitwip IA agency supports these projects: bitwip.ai (opens in a new tab)
