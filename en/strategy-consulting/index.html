02Method

One method, five steps

A brand, a campaign or a website: the path is the same, and you always know which step we’re at.

One method, five steps

  1. 01

    Step 1: Scoping

    We start by looking at where your communication stands.

    Your tools, your results, the competition: we examine everything before recommending anything. You receive an assessment, with the improvements to make first.

    We listen to your goals, challenge them and put them in writing.

    A kickoff meeting: your goals, your audiences, your timeline and budget constraints. If the situation calls for it, an audit of what already exists before any recommendation.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Strategy

    Positioning first, actions second.

    You decide on an action plan ranked by priority, scaled to your resources.

    A costed, prioritized recommendation you can decide on.

    Our people work together to design the strategy and the action plan.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Creative

    We write the documents that will guide what comes next.

    Brand platform, media plan, messaging: you review each document before it is finalized.

    In-house production, with clear approval points.

    Website, branding, communication campaigns: this is where your project comes to life.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Rollout

    The plan moves into execution, in-house or with us.

    Your teams can implement it on their own. We can also take over: content, campaigns, website or new brand guidelines.

    Launch, distribution, partner coordination.

    Regular check-ins until launch.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Measurement & review

    If we support you on what follows, we measure the plan against the results.

    We go back to the goals set at the start: what has been achieved, what remains to be done, which priorities to revisit.

    Results analyzed, adjustments proposed.

    Even after delivery, we look back. At the end of the engagement, we walk you through your project review.

How does a consulting engagement begin?

With an analysis of your context, your existing communications and your goals. We review your tools, your positioning against your competitors and your current results. You come away with a clear diagnosis and a list of improvements to make, ranked by priority.

What is a brand platform for?

It sets out what makes your brand stand apart from the others: its angle, its promise, its signature. This reference document then guides your visual identity and the way you speak. For the Rainbow Santé Group, born from a merger in French Guiana, the brand strategy went hand in hand with the redesign of its logo and identity.

Can you also put the strategy into action?

Yes, if you wish. Your teams can roll it out on their own, or we can take over: content, campaigns, website or new brand guidelines. In that case, we measure the plan against the results and review priorities with you.
